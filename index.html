01 · About

The story behind the data.

I often found myself asking: "What's the story behind the data? What decisions are we enabling?" That curiosity led me to Carnegie Mellon University.

The usefulness of a pot comes from its emptiness. — Lao Tzu

I've come to believe data works the same way. Its value isn't in the volume itself, but in the space it creates for better questions, sharper decisions, and the unexpected insights that surface when you let it breathe. The empty pot becomes a vessel; raw events become understanding.

What I value most, though, is the people on the other side of the work. The pipelines I'm proudest of weren't built alone — they came together through close collaboration, often at odd hours and over too much coffee. At CMU I got to extend that further as a Teaching Assistant for Machine Learning in Production / AI Engineering and Advanced Python, where the best moments were watching students click into hard ideas through conversation.

Whether it's a 3 AM incident thread or a whiteboard at midnight, I do my best work in good company.
