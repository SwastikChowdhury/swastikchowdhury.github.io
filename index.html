About

The story behind the data.

I often found myself asking: "What's the story behind the data? What decisions are we enabling?" That curiosity led me to Carnegie Mellon University.

The usefulness of a pot comes from its emptiness. — Lao Tzu

I've come to believe data works the same way. Its value isn't in the volume itself, but in the space it creates for better questions, sharper decisions, and the unexpected insights that surface when you let it breathe. The empty pot holds possibility; raw events hold the beginnings of understanding.

What I value most, though, is the people on the other side of the work. The work I am proudest of weren't built alone. They came together through close collaboration, often at odd hours and over too much coffee.

At CMU I got to extend that further as a Teaching Assistant for Machine Learning in Production and Advanced Python, where the best moments were helping students turn abstract ideas into working systems through conversations.

Whether it's a 3 AM incident thread or a whiteboard at midnight, I want to be there solving the problem.
